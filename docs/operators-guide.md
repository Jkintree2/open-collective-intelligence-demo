# Working notes for the back room and dictation

These notes cover the controls added for the demo. The complete handover guide will follow after testing.

## Dictation

If your browser offers Dictate, press it and speak. The button says “Listening… press to stop”. Press it again to stop, then check and edit the text before reading or posting it. If Dictate is absent or does not work, type your statement instead.

Desktop microphone checks and the iPhone Safari check are still pending. A check on a narrow desktop window does not establish that the phone keyboard or dictation works.

## The back room

Open `/admin`. Enter any username and your back room password. Keep that password to yourself. The page shows counts, the last recorded reading service problem and the latest fifty posts. The error line is cleared when the service restarts.

“Reload seed” adds missing seed statements without clearing your record. “Download a copy” saves the full record to your computer. Keep that file somewhere private.

“Reset to seed” clears the record and reloads the seed statements. Download a copy first. You must type RESET to confirm.

Deleting a post removes the statement and the claims, citations, proposals and evidence connections it created. An issue, solution or piece of evidence is removed only when it was touched by that post, is not a seed item and has no connections left. People and seed items stay. Links between solutions and issues, approvals, oppositions and the issue hierarchy stay too, even when they began with the deleted post.

A developer can restore a downloaded copy into an empty database. Restoring from this page is on the list for after the demo.

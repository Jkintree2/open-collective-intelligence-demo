# Handoff, 8 November 2026: v0.3, Write and Ask on one Home page

John's v0.3: one box for Write and Ask on a Home page, behind `HOME_ENABLED` (agreed 4 November; wording checks 5
November; his email of 7 November: one "Recent posts" list and "My posts"). Branch `v03-home`, merged on the
builder's go on 8 November 2026 with `HOME_ENABLED` unset, so the live site looks as it did until John sets it.

## Built

* **Home** (`/`, `app/home.py`, `app/routes_home.py`, `app/static/home.js`, `docs/planning/04_interface.md`, "v0.3:
  Write and Ask on one Home page"): one box; the site tells a statement from a question by rules, with no model call
  (`home.kind_of`), and asks back when it can't tell; a link sends the same words the other way. The small box on
  Issues, My posts and About takes its text to Home. Without JavaScript the form posts to `/home`.
* **Recent posts**: everyone's statements and questions mixed, newest first (`app/home_lists.py`, `_question.html`).
* **My posts** (`/my-posts`, "My statements" while Home is off): the member's own statements and questions mixed.
* **The list under the button** (`_home_mine.html`, also `GET /home/mine`): the member's five newest; app.js
  `refreshLists` draws it and the feed again after a post or an answer.
* **Who asked** (`docs/planning/03_schema.md`, "v0.3: who asked a question"): `ASKED` from an account to a `Question`,
  written only while Home is on for that member (Q56), read only for their own lists (Q57); never shown with a name.
  A back-room copy carries it.
* **Follow-ups**: a question with no words of its own ("Tell me more", "Why?") is answered but never kept or listed
  (`ask.has_own_words`); every list leaves out such questions kept before.

## Switching it on (John, in the Render dashboard)

1. Environment: add `HOME_ENABLED` = `preview` and `HOME_PREVIEW` = the account emails that should see it, separated by
   commas. Save; Render restarts the site.
2. When the wording is settled: `HOME_ENABLED` = `on` (every signed-in member). `off` or unset puts every page back.

The operator's guide has the same steps ("The Home page").

## Known

* Questions asked before Home was on for a member have no `ASKED`, so they are not on that member's My posts.
* To undo v0.3 once Home has been on, delete the `ASKED` edges first (`MATCH ()-[a:ASKED]->() DELETE a`): earlier code
  refuses a copy that holds them (`CLAUDE.md`).

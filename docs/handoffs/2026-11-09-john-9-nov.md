## What changed

* **Home** has no list of the writer's own under the button: `_home_mine.html`, `main._home_mine` and
  `GET /home/mine` are gone, and `app.js` draws only the feed again after a post or an answer. My posts keeps a
  member's own statements and questions (Q57 stays).
* **The About page's text** above the three documents is `app/templates/_about_site.html`, which `about.html`
  includes (the page is byte for byte as before). John edits the site's description there. `app/about_site.py`
  draws it with the site's switches (`site_context`) and turns it into plain words (`site_text`, through
  `fetch_page.html_text`).
* **Ask**, for a reader Home is on for (`site_questions`; added after the merge of PR 80 on the builder's go, so every other reader's Ask stays exactly as before), ends its instructions, while `ABOUT_ENABLED` is on, with `ask.ABOUT_RULES` and that text as a fourth
  document, D4 (`ask.system_prompt`). D4 may be cited only when the question, or an earlier one, names the site
  (`ask.names_site`, `ask.cited_documents`); a paragraph citing it needs no post, and its quotes must be in it. A
  record that holds nothing still gets a call for such a question, judged by the question a wordless follow-up
  refers to (`ask.answerable_without_record`, which now does the same for the guiding documents). A wordless
  follow-up to a question about the site stays on the site. D4 shows as "About this site." linked to `/about`
  (source kind `about`). If the About template will not draw, Ask logs `about_text_failed` and answers without D4.
  With `ABOUT_ENABLED` off the instructions are byte for byte as before.
* **Item pages**, with Home asking (`HOME_ENABLED` on or preview for the reader, and `ASK_ENABLED`): the box
  (`_write_about.html`, `_home_box.html` with `box_on`) takes the place of the link and posts the item to
  `POST /home`. Home then shows the database's summary of it (`main.about_for`, as `/?issue=` does; every summary
  now carries its `kind` and `key`), asks about it (`#home-ask` gets `data-<kind>`), carries it on in its own
  form (so it holds without JavaScript), and `home.js` puts `/?<kind>=<key>` in the address (`data-start`) so a
  reload sends nothing again. With Home or Ask off the link is byte for byte as before. The Ask box lower on these
  pages stays until John decides.
* `tests/fixtures/pages_before_v03/` holds John's About wording of 9 November. `scripts/asks.py` asks the cases
  with the About page on (`with_about`); cases S1 and S2 ask about the site.
